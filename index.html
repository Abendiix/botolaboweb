About BOTO LABO

BOTO LABO is a Sokoban-Puzzle game where Boto1, the protagonist, will need the help of their robot friends to complete the levels!

1

These robot friends are called Mibo2, and the only way they can complete the levels is by making them reach each level's flag3. To do so, you will have to move and turn them around to find the solution (which may be more than one!)4.

2

3

4 - There are 16 ways this level can be solved! (and yes, Boto can stretch their leg to infinity!)

While playing you will find different mechanics across the levels that will help you solve them and make the Mibos' interactions a lot more interesting! (hint: they get scared very easily!).
